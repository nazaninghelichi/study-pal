🧮 What it does

You set a daily goal — how many practice problems you're doing today — and log each one as you finish it. It tracks your streak, ranks you on a leaderboard, and reminds you if the number hasn't moved.

How it works

01Set a daily goal
02Log problems as you go
03Watch the streak build
04Get reminded if you stall
05Check the leaderboard

Two surfaces, one account

Same Postgres database underneath both — your goal and streak carry over no matter which one you open.

Philosophy

Most students don't fall behind because they don't understand the material — they fall behind because they stop showing up. Mathoclock exists to make showing up visible: a goal you committed to, a number that either moved today or didn't, a streak with something real to lose.

25%+ higher day-to-day follow-through observed among Mathoclock tutoring students using a daily accountability tool like this, vs. tracking nothing at all
