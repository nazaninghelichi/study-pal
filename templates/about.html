What this is

I built this for my own tutoring students, the ones who understand the material fine in our sessions and then disappear between them. This isn't about teaching you math. It's about making it obvious, to you and to someone who cares, whether you actually did the practice today or just meant to.

Set how many problems you're doing today, log each one as you finish it, and the app keeps a streak going for as long as you show up. When you stall, it says so.

Nobody signs themselves up, on either side. I add every student myself, and an accountability buddy has to verify themselves by email before anything they see or confirm actually counts.

How to use it

1Get added. I add your email so you can sign in with just that, no password to invent, and access is invite-only for students I'm actually working with.
2Set today's goal. How many problems, updateable anytime from Settings.
3Log as you go. Every problem you finish, tap it in, and the count and streak update right away.
4Run a focus session. Open Pomewdoro, pick a length, and study while a cat slowly grows on screen. Finish the session and it bursts into hearts you tap to collect.
5Spend your hearts. Repair a broken streak with them, or send some to a friend who's having a rough week.
6Add an accountability buddy in Settings, a parent, a friend, whoever will actually check on you. They get an email asking them to verify themselves first, and nothing starts until they do.
7Your buddy confirms your count and can send you a sticker for it. It lands straight in your collection.
8Check the leaderboard. It only shows students whose buddy confirmed the day before. No confirmation, no ranking, so nobody can just type in a big number.

Two ways in, one account

Telegram bot or the website, same account underneath, same Postgres database. Log a problem on your phone during a break, check your streak on a laptop later. It's all just there.

❤️ Hearts

You earn hearts by finishing a full Pomewdoro session, not by logging problems. The cat grows the whole time you're focused, and finishing bursts it into hearts you tap to catch before they drift off screen.

Once you have some, you can spend them two ways: repair a broken streak (costs {{ streak_repair_cost }}, only works on yesterday, not today), or send some straight to a friend who's having a rough week. Both happen right on the Pomewdoro or leaderboard page.

🏆 Leaderboard

This isn't a raw ranking of who typed in the biggest number. It only shows yesterday's results, and only for students whose accountability buddy actually confirmed the count. No buddy, or a buddy who hasn't confirmed yet, means you're just not on the board that day, not ranked at zero.

That's on purpose. A self-reported number anyone could inflate isn't worth ranking people by. A buddy-confirmed one is.

Why it exists

Most of the students I tutor don't struggle with the material, we sort that out fine in session. What gets people is the space between sessions, when nobody's watching and it's easy to tell yourself you'll catch up tomorrow. This app doesn't study for you. It just refuses to let "I've been working on it" stay vague.
